Scribb: sketchbooks and journals you can turn the pages of

Scribb is a free home for sketchbooks and journals. Fill a tactile digital book with your drawings, paintings and words, choose its cover and binding, then keep it private or publish it so anyone can turn its pages.

What you can make

  • Sketchbooks with up to 40 artworks, on single pages or full two-page spreads, in Linen, Spiral or Watercolor bindings.
  • Journals with 18 page layouts, pictures, stickers, scribbles and voice typing.
  • Ask my journal, a companion that answers questions about your own entries.
  • A public profile at scribb.xyz/@yourname that shows every book you publish.

How it works

  1. Create a free account with a username or with Google.
  2. Start a sketchbook or a journal and fill its pages.
  3. Publish it when you are ready. Every new book stays private until you do.
