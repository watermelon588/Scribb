About Scribb

Scribb is a free home for digital sketchbooks and journals. You fill a book that turns its pages like paper, with a cover, a binding and your own art or words, then keep it to yourself or publish it to your profile at scribb.xyz/@yourname.

Start your first book

Who it is for

Scribb is for illustrators, painters, students and anyone who keeps a sketchbook or a diary and wants a digital one that still feels made by hand. It suits a finished portfolio as well as a messy daily practice.

What you can make

Scribb makes two kinds of book: sketchbooks for drawings and paintings, and journals for writing. Both open, close and turn their pages like a real notebook.

  • Sketchbooks with up to 40 artworks, on single pages or full two-page spreads across the fold.
  • Three bindings: Linen, Spiral and Watercolor, with an optional elastic closure and a cover caption of up to 48 characters.
  • Journals with dated entries and 18 page layouts in four groups: Minimal, Long, Layered and Spread.
  • Pictures, stickers and scribbles on journal pages, plus voice typing where your browser supports it.
  • Ask my journal, a companion that answers questions about your own entries.

How it works

Making a book takes four steps, and nothing is public until you choose to publish it.

  1. Create a free account with a username and password or with Google.
  2. Choose a sketchbook or a journal, a cover and a format, and fill the pages.
  3. Save as often as you like. The book stays private.
  4. Publish it when you are ready. It appears on your profile and in Explore, where people can like it, save it and follow you.

Privacy and your work

New books are private by default, and a private book answers "not found" to everyone but its owner. Uploaded images are re-encoded, which removes camera and location metadata. Scribb asks AI training crawlers not to collect its pages; AI search assistants may still read public pages so they can link people to them.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what people ask most before they start their first book.

Is Scribb free?

Yes. Creating an account, making sketchbooks and journals and publishing them is free.

Who can see my books?

Only you. Every new book is private until you publish it. Published books appear on your profile and in Explore.

What can I put in a sketchbook?

Up to 40 artworks per book as JPG, PNG or WebP images of up to 10 MB each, on single pages or across a full two-page spread.

How is a journal different from a sketchbook?

A journal is for writing. It has dated entries, 18 page layouts, one font and ink for the whole book, pictures, stickers, scribbles and voice typing.

Is my art used to train AI?

Scribb tells AI training crawlers not to collect its pages. AI search assistants may read public pages so they can link to them. Ask my journal reads only your own entries, and only when you use it.
